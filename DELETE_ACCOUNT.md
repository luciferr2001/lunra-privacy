# Delete your Lunra account and data

Lunra (developed by Rutik Rathod, contact: rathodrutik05@gmail.com) lets
you request deletion of your account and cloud-backed-up data in two
ways.

## In the app

1. Open Lunra and go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Backup**, tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm, and sign in again with Google if asked (Google requires a
   fresh sign-in before allowing account deletion).

This permanently deletes your Firebase account link and every entry
backed up under it (periods, symptoms, mood, notes, energy, settings).
Data stored locally on your device is not affected by this — see
"What stays" below.

## Without the app

Email the developer at rathodrutik05@gmail.com from the Google account
you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Lunra data." Your
cloud-backed-up data and account link will be deleted within 30 days,
and you'll get a confirmation reply.

## What gets deleted

- Your Firebase Authentication account record.
- Every entry synced to your cloud backup: period dates, symptoms, mood,
  energy, notes, and app settings.

## What stays

- Nothing you logged is deleted from your device by this process — Lunra
  is local-first, and what's on your device is controlled entirely by
  you (delete individual entries in-app, or uninstall the app to remove
  everything local).
- If you never signed in with Google, there is no account or cloud data
  to delete — everything you've logged already exists only on your
  device.
